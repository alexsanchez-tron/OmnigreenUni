Omnigreen: Perform Inventory SKU Audits Click here to watch Quick guidde This training will equip you with the skills to conduct thorough inventory audits using the Omnigreen Back Office system. Go to backoffice.omnigreen.ai 1. Access Inventory Audit Module You will learn how to start, manage, and complete audits to ensure accurate stock records. Click "Inventory Audit v2" to open the inventory audit module within the Back Office application. Access Inventory Audit Module 2. Initiate New Inventory Audit Click "Start New Audit" to begin a new inventory audit session in the system. Initiate New Inventory Audit 3. Enter SKU Barcode Scan the units source ID to identify the product for auditing. Enter SKU Barcode 4. Add SKU to Audit List Select "Add" to include the scanned skew in the current audit list. Add SKU to Audit List 5. Room Next select the room or rooms you will be auditing in Room 6. Begin Inventory Audit Click "Start Audit" to officially start the inventory auditing process for the selected items. Begin Inventory Audit 7. Re-enter SKU Barcode Scan the Unit to scan individual units to confirm the product during unit scanning. Re-enter SKU Barcode 8. Hand Count If this is a quick hand count, select the hand count toggle. Hand Count 9. Select Quantity Fill here with the quantity count for hand counted units to update inventory records. Select Quantity 10. Finalize Audit Adjustments Click "Adjust And Complete" to apply changes and complete the current audit entry. Finalize Audit Adjustments 11. Mark Inventory as Completed The "Completed" marker will indicate that the inventory audit for this item is finished. Mark Inventory as Completed 12. Choose Audit Style Audit Style is the preferred style or method for conducting the audit. Choose Audit Style 13. Editable Section Access the action menu. Editable Section 14. Continue Audit Editing Click "Edit / Continue Audit" to resume making changes to the ongoing inventory audit. Or Cancel to stop and release the items state. Continue Audit Editing 15. Enter Adjustment Reason For a discrepancy of an item, for whatever reason will need to be entered. Do so here. Enter Adjustment Reason 16. Input Reason Quantity Click "1" to specify the quantity related to the adjustment reason entered. A remark next to the reason must be entered in order to continue. Input Reason Quantity 17. Save Audit Details Click "Save Detail" to save all entered details and changes made during the audit. Save Audit Details Powered by guidde
